Method — identical to the 13-partner report. Internet complaints = SERVICE_TICKET_MODEL tickets whose latest title starts "Internet", by ticket-added month (UTC→IST). A ticket is outage-driven — and removed — if it lands inside a confirmed >3-device incident window (INCIDENTS, first-fail → min(close, first-fail + 4h)); tiny ≤3-device outages stay in the ratio. Outage data starts 8 Jan 2026, so Nov and Jan 1–7 are estimated with each CSP's own removal rate from the Jan–May steady-state months (the Jun–Aug upgrade-wave months are excluded from the rate — the upgrade work's own mass outages would otherwise over-delete Nov tickets). Userbase = active customers on the CSP's devices (same source as the audit dashboard, pulled 19 Aug), applied to all months. Aug* = 1–18 Aug pro-rated to a full month. Reconciliation with the 13-partner report's old "Pre 46%": that figure was the Nov–Dec mean for its 13 CSPs computed on the early-July incidents data; raw ticket counts reproduce exactly today (Nov = 1,008 tickets = 58.7% raw), but the outage-detection history has since been reprocessed and now marks more Jan–May tickets as outage-driven, so the same 13 CSPs' Nov estimate reads 42.2% on current data (the main report itself now frames Pre as Apr–May 29.2%).
